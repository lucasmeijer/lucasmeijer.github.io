Narrated videos as a form factor.

It took me some time to figure out what matters in a good narrated video.

For me, it’s:

  • Karaoke-style words.
  • The words have to be at the top, not the bottom. Otherwise the playback controls hide them.
  • Text-to-speech is nice to have, but the words are what matter.

Useful for bug reports, demos, and end-user testing.
